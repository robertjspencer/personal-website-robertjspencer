Google Tasks Connector

A personal integration for managing Robert Spencer’s Google Tasks through Composio and an AI assistant.

Overview Privacy policy Terms of service

What it does

The connector lets Robert use ChatGPT or Claude to read task lists and tasks, create tasks, update their details, mark them complete, and delete them. Composio facilitates the connection to Google Tasks.

Personal use

This integration is operated by Robert Spencer for his own account. It is not offered as a public sign-up service. Access to Google Tasks requires separate permission through Google’s authorization process.

Your data and control

Task information passes through Composio and the assistant used for a request. Read the privacy policy for details about access, storage, sharing, and removing access, and the terms of service for conditions of use.

These informational pages do not connect to Google Tasks or ask for a Google password.
