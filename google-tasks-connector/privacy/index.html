Who operates this integration

Robert Spencer operates Google Tasks Connector for personal use with his own Google account. This policy covers the connector and these informational pages.

Information accessed and its purpose

After Google authorization, the connector can access task lists and task information, including titles, notes, due dates, completion status, and identifiers. It uses this information to carry out task-management requests: reading, creating, updating, completing, and deleting tasks and managing task lists.

Composio manages authorization credentials needed to maintain the connection. These credentials and task contents are not published on this website.

How information is shared

Google provides the task data. Composio processes requests and results between Google Tasks and the assistant used for a request: ChatGPT (OpenAI) or Claude (Anthropic). Task content sent to an assistant may become part of its conversation history. Data is not automatically sent to both assistants simply because they are listed here.

Providers may process information using their service providers under their respective terms. See Composio’s privacy policy, OpenAI’s privacy policy, and Anthropic’s privacy policy.

Storage and retention

Task records remain in Google Tasks unless changed or deleted. Composio may retain connection credentials and tool-execution logs; those logs may contain request or response data. ChatGPT or Claude may retain conversations containing task information. Retention and any use for model improvement depend on the provider, account type, and settings. This policy does not promise zero retention or a blanket provider no-training guarantee.

These informational pages have no task database, forms, analytics scripts, or browser storage. Cloudflare serves the pages and may process ordinary web-request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, for delivery and security. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Limits on use

Robert uses Google Tasks information for personal task management, not to sell data, serve advertisements, or train a general-purpose AI model. The connector’s use and transfer of Google API data must comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Connected services must be configured and used consistently with those requirements.

Removing access and deleting information

Access can be revoked through Google Account connections. Disconnect the account in Composio as well to stop using the connection there.

Revoking access stops future authorized access through that grant; it does not erase information already retained by providers. Delete tasks in Google Tasks and use Composio, ChatGPT, or Claude account controls or support channels to request deletion of retained logs or conversations. Provider retention rules and any required legal retention may still apply.

Contact and updates

Questions or deletion requests relating to Robert’s operation of the connector can be sent to rspencer9721@gmail.com. This policy will be updated if the connector’s data practices change.
